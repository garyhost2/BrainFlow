\section{Generative model}

Everything lives on the unit sphere $\Sph^{d-1}\subset\R^{d}$, $d=256$. Write
$\Pi_z v=v-\langle v,z\rangle z$ for the tangent projection at $z$ and
$\exp_z(w)=\cos\|w\|\,z+\sin\|w\|\,w/\|w\|$ for the exponential map, applied to $w\leftarrow\Pi_zw$
and returning $z$ when $\|w\|<10^{-6}$.

Four scalars parameterise the world: the resultant $A=0.37$, the private-factor weight
$\beta=0.8$, the source tilt $j=0.15$ rad, and the coupling $\rho=1$. Write $s=\sqrt{1-\beta^2}=0.6$.

One draw is produced as follows.
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep1pt
\item $c\sim\mathrm{Unif}(\Sph^{d-1})$.
\item $\xi_s$ a uniform unit vector in the tangent space at $c$.
\item $\xi_t=\xi_s$ when $\rho=1$; otherwise
$\xi_t=\Pi_c\big(\rho\,\xi_s+\sqrt{1-\rho^2}\,\xi'\big)$ normalised, with $\xi'$ an independent
uniform unit tangent vector. Both conditional marginals are invariant in $\rho$; only the
dependence between the source-side and target-side private factors changes. Measured drift of the
target mean over the whole $\rho$ sweep: $2.86\times10^{-4}$.
\item $m=\big(s\,c+\beta\,\xi_t\big)/\|\cdot\|$.
\item $z_1\sim\mathrm{vMF}(m,\kappa)$.
\item $\varphi=j+\sigma_{\mathrm{pol}}\,\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,1)$, clamped to
$(10^{-3},\pi-10^{-3})$; $z_0=\big(\cos\varphi\,c+\sin\varphi\,\xi_s\big)/\|\cdot\|$.
\item Optionally $z_0\leftarrow\mathrm{slerp}(u,z_0,\lambda)$ with $u$ uniform on the sphere. This
is the noise-to-informative dial $\lambda$ used only in the guidance stage.
\end{enumerate}

$\sigma_{\mathrm{pol}}=0$ is the default and makes $z_0$ a deterministic function of $(c,\xi_s)$.
This is the degenerate source: the conditional law of $z_0$ given $c$ is supported on a
$(d-2)$-sphere of radius $\sin j$, one dimension short of the target support.

\subsection*{Concentration}

$\kappa$ is solved from $A$ through $A_d(\kappa)=I_{d/2}(\kappa)/I_{d/2-1}(\kappa)$, evaluated by
a backward continued fraction with $\max(500,\lceil 2.2\kappa+100\rceil)$ levels and inverted by
$140$ bisection steps on a bracket doubled until it contains the root. At $d=256$, $A=0.37$ this
gives $\kappa=109.64$.

Sampling uses Wood's rejection method. The acceptance test is evaluated in float64. To keep the
sampler free of host-device synchronisation the implementation draws $4B$ proposals in one shot
and selects $B$ of them by \texttt{topk} on the acceptance indicator perturbed by
$10^{-3}\mathcal U$, rather than looping until $B$ are accepted; the loop variant requires reading
an accepted count back to the host every iteration. Verification at the operating point: target
$A=0.37$, measured $\hat A=0.3707$ over $2\times10^{4}$ draws.

\section{Architecture}

\subsection*{Velocity field}

\texttt{FieldNet}, used in every continuous stage.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Component & Specification & Initialisation\\
\midrule
time embedding & $64$ features, $\omega_k=\exp\!\big(\tfrac{k}{31}\log 200\big)$, $k=0..31$,
  output $[\sin(t\omega),\cos(t\omega)]$ & fixed buffer\\
time projection & $\mathrm{Linear}(64,W)$ & default\\
input & $\mathrm{Linear}(d\cdot n_{\mathrm{in}},W)$, $n_{\mathrm{in}}\in\{2,3\}$ & default\\
trunk & $3\times$ residual block, below & --\\
output & $\mathrm{Linear}(W,d)$ & zeros\\
null condition & one learned vector in $\R^{d}$ & zeros\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

$n_{\mathrm{in}}=2$ concatenates $(z_t,c)$; $n_{\mathrm{in}}=3$ concatenates $(z_t,c,a)$ where $a$
is the auxiliary input defined in Section~\ref{sec:aux}. Default width $W=512$; the width sweep
uses $W\in\{256,512,1024,2048\}$.

Each residual block is adaptive-LayerNorm-zero:
\[
h\ \leftarrow\ h+g\odot\mathrm{MLP}\big(\mathrm{LN}(h)\odot(1+s_c)+s_h\big),\qquad
(s_h,s_c,g)=\mathrm{Linear}(W,3W)\big(\mathrm{SiLU}(e)\big),
\]
with $\mathrm{LN}$ a LayerNorm without learnable affine parameters, $\mathrm{MLP}=
\mathrm{Linear}(W,4W)\to\mathrm{SiLU}\to\mathrm{Linear}(4W,W)$, and $e$ the projected time
embedding. The modulation layer is zero-initialised, so at step zero every block is the identity
and the network output is exactly zero.

Classifier-free guidance replaces $c$ by the learned null vector on a Bernoulli($p$) subset of the
batch, $p=0.1$ where used, and samples with
$v=v_\varnothing+w\,(v_c-v_\varnothing)$.

\subsection*{Population-field oracle}

\texttt{OraNet}, a $2\to96\to96\to1$ MLP with SiLU activations, consumed as
$v(z,t)=\mathrm{OraNet}(\langle z,c\rangle,t)\,\Pi_z c$. This is the exact functional form of the
marginal field under azimuth conservation, so it isolates the population field from any
approximation error in a general network. Trained $3500$ steps, batch $2048$, AdamW at
$2\times10^{-3}$; $1800$ steps and batch $1024$ inside the two sweeps.

\subsection*{Masked-diffusion denoiser}

For the discrete stage. Tokens $L=16$, vocabulary $V=12$ plus one mask symbol, $K=8$ conditions,
$M=32$ private variants per condition, per-token corruption $\eta=0.05$, extra source corruption
$\gamma=0.35$, and a fraction $\beta_d$ of positions carrying the private factor. The denoiser is
a $3$-layer MLP of width $256$ on one-hot inputs, predicting all $L$ token distributions.

\subsection*{Checkpoint selection}

Selecting on the training loss is invalid here: the loss and sampled quality move in opposite
directions along every knob swept (Spearman $+0.943$ between terminal loss and fidelity across the
$\alpha$ family). Where early stopping is enabled, checkpoints are scored every
$\max(200,\lfloor\text{steps}/8\rfloor)$ steps by a sampled distributional criterion --- the
one-dimensional Wasserstein distance of the polar coordinate against a fresh draw from the true
conditional, at $6$ conditions $\times$ $64$ samples, fixed evaluation seed $99$ --- and the best
is restored. Selection and reporting use disjoint seeds ($99$ against $1234$).

\section{Integration solver}

Heun, second order, intrinsic to the sphere. With a uniform grid $t_0<\dots<t_K$ on $[t_\ast,1]$
and $\Delta=t_{i+1}-t_i$:
\[
v_1=f(z,t_i),\quad
\tilde z=\exp_z(\Delta v_1),\quad
v_2=\Pi_z f(\tilde z,t_{i+1}),\quad
z\leftarrow\exp_z\!\big(\tfrac{\Delta}{2}(v_1+v_2)\big).
\]
The corrector velocity is transported back to $z$ before averaging, so both slopes are added in
one tangent space. Default $K=32$; the step sweep uses $K\in\{1,2,4,8,16,32,64\}$.

Two solver-level knobs are exposed. Churn adds $\exp_z\!\big(\zeta\sqrt{\Delta}\,u\big)$ after each
step with $u$ a uniform unit tangent vector, $\zeta\in\{0,0.05,0.15\}$. Truncation starts the
integration at $t_\ast>0$ instead of $0$, which is the direct probe of the $1/t$ amplification:
the predicted error scales with $\int_{t_\ast}^{1}dt/t$, and the measured endpoint error tracks
that integral at Spearman $0.964$.

The flat-space endpoint map of Section~\ref{sec:flat} is integrated separately, in float64, by the
same Heun rule applied to the matrix ODE $\dot M=K(t)M$, $M(0)=I$, with $2000$ steps and
$K(t)=G(t)V(t)^{-1}$ obtained by a linear solve rather than an explicit inverse. Euler at the same
step count leaves a discretisation floor of $3.4\times10^{-5}$, which is larger than the effect
being measured; Heun lowers it to $1.2\times10^{-7}$.

\section{Evaluation protocol}
\label{sec:proto}

Two disjoint instruments, because they disagree and the disagreement is a result.

\emph{Fidelity.} $768$ conditions, $6$ samples each. Reports mean cosine to a matched target draw,
alignments to $c$, $\xi_s$, $\xi_t$, $m$, the in-plane energy
$\E[\langle z,c\rangle^2+\langle z,\xi_s\rangle^2]$, the output polar spread, and the mean
off-diagonal pairwise cosine among the $6$ samples sharing a condition.

\emph{Distributional.} $16$ conditions, $128$ samples each, evaluation seed $1234$. Reports the
one-dimensional Wasserstein distance of the polar coordinate, the energy distance, and $5$-nearest-
neighbour coverage, each against a fresh draw from the true conditional. A second independent
draw supplies a null for all three, so every number has a floor attached: the polar Wasserstein
null is $0.00967$.

Cosine to a single target is a mean-seeking score and ranks a correct sampler last. Measured: the
true posterior scores $0.0500$, below returning the source unchanged ($0.2624$), below the constant
map $z\mapsto c$ ($0.2221$), and below every trained model. This is reported as a property of the
metric, not of the models, and it is why no claim in this work rests on cosine alone.

\subsection{Degenerate source, restored}

Sweeping $\sigma_{\mathrm{pol}}$ from $0$ to $0.4$ at $d=256$ with $1.2\times10^{5}$ samples per
setting. At $\sigma_{\mathrm{pol}}=0$ the source variance along $c$ is $7.08\times10^{-24}$,
numerically rank-deficient; any $\sigma_{\mathrm{pol}}>0$ restores it, the smallest tested giving
$5.58\times10^{-7}$. The polar gain matches the ratio of target to source polar spreads to within
$0.71\%$ across the sweep and diverges as $\sigma_{\mathrm{pol}}\to0$, reaching $73.6$ at
$\sigma_{\mathrm{pol}}=0.005$. The mean polar landing is $As$ to within $4.07\times10^{-4}$ at
every $\sigma_{\mathrm{pol}}$, so the singular limit identifies the collapse point rather than
destroying it. The pencil determinant is nonzero throughout and changes sign inside the sweep,
between $\sigma_{\mathrm{pol}}=0.03$ and $0.06$.

Three statements, of which two survive and one does not.

\emph{Survives.} The sign is the same in all eight cells, at $15$ to $147$ standard errors.

\emph{Survives.} Capacity does not close the gap at either learning rate. At $\eta=10^{-3}$ the
change from width $256$ to $2048$ is $+0.0044$ against a two-standard-error tolerance of $0.0129$,
that is, flat. At $\eta=10^{-4}$ it is $+0.0189$ against a tolerance of $0.0024$: the gap
significantly \emph{widens} with capacity. Over the same range the training loss falls
($-3.45\times10^{-4}$ and $-2.03\times10^{-4}$) and the distributional error falls ($-0.0422$ and
$-0.0098$). The model is better optimised and distributionally better at larger width while the
gap does not close. This is the form of the argument that the difference is not estimation error,
and it is the only form of it that the data support.

\emph{Does not survive.} The gap is not independent of the learning rate. It is $1.6$ to $2.2$
times larger at $\eta=10^{-3}$ than at $\eta=10^{-4}$, far outside the standard errors. The
registered prediction that the magnitude is a tuning-independent constant is contradicted. What
may be claimed is the sign and the failure to close; the effect size must be reported per
learning rate.

Maximal-update transfer is unresolved. The distributional criterion selects $10^{-3}$ at width
$256$ and $10^{-4}$ at width $2048$; the criterion is nearly flat, at $[0.0744,0.0458]$ and
$[0.0322,0.0360]$ respectively, so the selection is weak rather than a clean failure. The standard-
parameterisation control behaves worse on every axis --- loss rises with width, distributional
error rises with width, and the width effect falls inside seed noise --- which is why maximal-
update parameterisation is used for the headline numbers.

\subsection{Mechanism and remedies}

The deficit is attacked at the singularity. Truncating the integration at $t_\ast$ recovers most of
it, $0.2166\to0.2997$ at $t_\ast=0.2$, and the recovery tracks the amplification integral at
Spearman $0.964$. Auxiliary noise recovers it fully, $0.2149\to0.3105$ at $\sigma_a=1$, reaching
the marginal model's $0.3180$ to within $0.008$. Churn does not help, which distinguishes an
ill-conditioned recovery map from a variance problem. Along the $\alpha$ family the terminal loss
and the sampled fidelity move together at Spearman $0.943$ --- the loss rises as quality rises.

\subsection{Threshold}

crossing of the flow against the do-nothing baseline, over $36$ cells. Maximum absolute error
$0.0334$ on the $27$ cells where both exist.

\section{Cluster experiments}

Two further runs on NVIDIA A100 80GB nodes, PyTorch $2.1.0{+}\mathrm{cu}121$. Job $381574$
carries the criterion to dimension $64$; job $381593$, a three-task array, carries the degenerate

\subsection{The criterion at scale}
\label{sec:thmscale}

\subsubsection*{Design}

Random systems rather than hand-built ones. For each dimension $n\in\{2,4,8,16,32,64\}$ and each
of six independent repetitions, $2048$ pairs of marginals are drawn as
$\Sigma=AA^{\T}/n+0.35\,I$ with $A$ standard normal, and thirty-six coupling configurations are
built on each pair, all in float64.

A clean coupling is $C_0=\tfrac12(\sigma\Sigma_0+\tau\Sigma_1)$ with
$\sigma,\tau\sim\mathrm{Unif}(0.30,0.55)$, which satisfies both hypotheses by construction. It is
then perturbed in two independent directions:
\[
S=\tfrac12(C+C^{\T})\ \leftarrow\ S_{\mathrm{clean}}+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{off}}R,\qquad
C\ \leftarrow\ \tfrac12 S+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{skew}}W,
\]
with $R$ symmetric and Gram--Schmidt projected out of $\mathrm{span}\{\Sigma_0,\Sigma_1\}$ in the
Frobenius inner product, $W$ skew, both rescaled to $\|S_{\mathrm{clean}}\|_F$. Each
$\varepsilon$ ranges over $\{0,0.01,0.03,0.1,0.3,1.0\}$. Joint positive semidefiniteness is
enforced by shrinking $C$ until $\|\Sigma_0^{-1/2}C\Sigma_1^{-1/2}\|_2\le0.92$.

The endpoint map is integrated as in \S\ref{sec:flat}, Heun at $2000$ steps with a linear solve
in place of an explicit inverse. The integrator floor is measured rather than assumed: the clean
coupling is rebuilt at shrink margin $0.55$ instead of $0.92$ and the resulting map displacement
is recorded. At $n=64$ that floor is $1.6\times10^{-8}$ and marginal matching holds at
$1.5\times10^{-7}$.

tested, violated or not, confirming that the corresponding proposition carries no hypothesis on
the skew part.

The two hypotheses are not interchangeable. At unit relative violation a skew violation displaces

$6.585$. The register carried the prediction that the two are comparably fragile; it is
contradicted, and this is the one contradiction of the six. The asymmetry reproduces --- an
earlier independent run at smaller scale gave $6.75$.

\subsection{Super-resolution as an instance}
\label{sec:srscale}

\subsubsection*{The instance}

$c$ is the class label, $z_1$ the image, and $z_0=\mathsf P z_1$ with $\mathsf P=\mathsf{UD}$,
$\mathsf D$ average pooling by $f=4$ and $\mathsf U$ nearest-neighbour upsampling. $\mathsf P$ is
an exact projection, verified numerically: the identity arm carries relative null-space energy
$7.5\times10^{-15}$. Conditioned on $c$ the source is a deterministic function of the target, so
the endpoints are maximally dependent, which is what the setting requires and what an independent
source excludes.

STL-10, $5000$ training and $1024$ test images, resized and centre-cropped to each resolution and
normalised to $[-1,1]$. Backbone is a compact UNet: channel multipliers $(1,2,2)$ on base $64$,
two residual blocks per level, GroupNorm with $8$ groups, time and class embeddings combined into
a per-block scale and shift, and a zero-initialised output convolution. Input channels are three
for the marginal model and six for the source-conditioned and regression arms. AdamW at
$2\times10^{-4}$ with one-cycle schedule, $20{,}000$ steps, batch $64$, gradient clipping at
$1.0$, bfloat16 autocast, three seeds. Sampling is Heun at $32$ steps on the linear interpolant.

\subsubsection*{The degenerate source, measured}

$\operatorname{rank}\operatorname{Cov}(z_0\mid c)\le r=3(\mathrm{res}/f)^2$. Rank is estimated
from the Gram matrix in float64 with strictly more samples than the bound, so no figure is
sample-limited.

\midrule
$32$ & $3072$ & $192$ & $192$ & $1023$ & $1024$\\

Exact equality with the bound at every resolution, against target ranks that are sample-limited
full. The source support therefore has strictly smaller dimension than the target support, a

``null'' is restored high-frequency energy as a fraction of the target's. The flow beats the

\subsubsection*{What does not transfer}

The ordering of the two models reverses here, and the three contradictions of this run are all
this prediction. $\mathcal M_2$ scores higher at every resolution, by $0.114$, $0.062$ and
$0.017$, the last inside one standard error, while restoring $17.5\%$, $12.3\%$ and $11.2\%$ less
high-frequency energy. The two gaps move oppositely with resolution: the score gap collapses
while the energy gap does not.

Between two arms of identical capacity and training that differ only in whether the network
receives $z_0$, a higher score with less restored detail is the mean-seeking inversion of
\S\ref{sec:proto}, not a better model. It is reported as a third instance of that inversion and
not as support for the ordering, which is scoped to the sphere.

One limit on that reading. The restored-energy fraction is not a collapse index on its own: the
regression arm records the highest fraction, $0.68$ to $0.78$, together with the lowest score of
the three learned arms, so the statistic also counts incorrect high-frequency energy. The
comparison is sound between arms of matched capacity and training, which is why it is drawn for
$\mathcal M_1$ against $\mathcal M_2$ and for nothing else. A distributional metric on the
restoration outputs would settle the ordering. None was run.

\subsection{The loss floor and the endpoint map decouple}

A closed-form computation, not a cluster run, reported here because it is the consequence the
criterion carries for practice. For jointly Gaussian endpoints under the linear interpolant the
irreducible flow-matching loss is available in closed form,
\[
\mathcal F=\int_0^1\operatorname{Tr}\Big(\operatorname{Var}(z_1-z_0)-G(t)V(t)^{-1}G(t)^{\T}\Big)dt,
\]
with $V$ and $G$ as in \S\ref{sec:flat}. This is the same object reported elsewhere as the
Markovization gap. Sweeping coupling strength along arms that satisfy the criterion and along
arms that violate it, over $47$ random systems in dimensions $3$ and $6$:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrr@{}}
\toprule
& map displacement, median & loss floor ratio, median\\
\midrule
criterion satisfied & $3.17\times10^{-4}$ & $2.23\times$ \quad $[2.14,\,2.41]$\\
criterion violated & $6.35\times10^{-1}$ & $1.23\times$ \quad $[1.07,\,1.99]$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The couplings that are provably equivalent at the endpoint produce the larger spread in training
loss; the couplings that move the endpoint map by a median $63\%$ produce the smaller. The
training loss is therefore not merely uninformative about the endpoint map, it is ordered against
it, and it cannot be used to select a coupling. This is consistent with, and independent of, the
measured anti-correlation between terminal loss and sampled quality on the sphere.

Four entries recorded as contradictions do not measure what their \texttt{predicts} string

the two auxiliary families trace one curve &
